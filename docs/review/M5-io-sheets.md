### ERC warnings (8), explained

All `ground_pin_not_ground`: the two from M3 (U201, U203) plus the field-side ground pins named `GNDB` of U602, U701 and U702, which sit on `0V_IO`. KiCad only accepts net names containing "GND" as grounds; the plan fixes the field return as `0V_IO`. The connections are correct (verified from the netlist).

## What is on the sheets

**5 Inputs.** One 4-channel sheet (one TLP290-4) used five times: DI1-4, DI5-8, SEL1-4, SEL5-8, SEL9-12. Per channel a 3-pole terminal (+24 V, IN, 0 V), 4.7 k 1206 and 10 nF across the AC-input LED, collector to the MCU pin and to a green LED through a 1 k array element to +3V3. The parent sheet holds, per group (DI and SEL), the 0.5 A PTC for the sensor +24 V and the COM jumper (1-2 NPN, 2-3 PNP, shunt fitted 2-3), plus the 20 plug accessories. References run R501 to R520, J501 to J520, U501 to U505.

**6 MPG.** J601 (+5V_IO through a 0.25 A PTC, 0V_IO, A, A-bar, B, B-bar), PSM712 per pair with an unfitted 120 R across it, AM26LS32A (G high, ~G low, unused receivers grounded), CA-IS3742LN: reverse channels carry A and B to PB4/PB5, forward channel 1 carries the relay output enable `OE_FIELD`.

**7 Relays.** DO1-8 through two CA-IS3740LN (enable pin = `OE_FIELD`, 100 k pull-downs on the driver inputs), ULN2803A with COM on +24V_IO, 8 sockets P2R-05P, ON LED with 22 k across each coil, COM through a Littelfuse fuse block to a 3-pole terminal (COM, NO, NC), unfitted RC snubber footprint across each contact. Relays and T5A fuses (bought in India) are accessory symbols marked DNP, as are the fuse covers and plugs. The coil and contact sides of each socket are separated by a dashed barrier.

**8 Analog.** CA-IS3740HW (default high) to MCP4822 on +5V_A (78L05 from +15V_A), 2N7002DW clamp, LM2904 with gain 1 + 14.7 k/10.0 k, 100 R inside the loop, SMAJ15A and 10 nF, one 2-pole terminal per output.

## Library changes

- `scripts/relayout_symbols.py` now also re-lays out: P2R-05P socket (coil left, contacts right), CA-IS3740LN/HW and CA-IS3742LN (supply and enable pins together at the top, grounds together at the bottom, signal rows aligned across the barrier; the 3742 has the field side on the left), AM26LS32A (inputs left, outputs right), 2N7002DW, L78L05, MCP4822, and LM2904 as two op-amp triangles plus a power unit. Pin numbers are unchanged; the datasheet pin check is still clean for all 44 symbols.
- `lib/lcec.kicad_sym` gains `R_Pack04_H` (resistor array drawn horizontally; pins 1-4 right, 8-5 left, pairs 1-8, 2-7, 3-6, 4-5 as in the footprint).
- Hierarchical sheet reuse: `scripts/schgen.py` writes one instance entry per use, with its own references.

## BOM changes

All "est" quantities are now final and equal the placed counts: 100 nF 40 (was 47), 4.7 uF 1 (was 3), 10 k 7 (was 10), 1 k status 5 (was 7), 1 uF 4 (was 6). Everything else was already exact. Still open: the Omron P2R-05P socket (C1524860) shows 44 in stock on lcsc.com for 8 needed.

## Please confirm

1. **RC snubbers across the relay contacts** (unfitted): I drew footprint-only parts, 100 R in a 2512 and 47 nF X2 in a 18 x 8 mm 15 mm-pitch film capacitor footprint, as a typical 230 VAC snubber. The plan says "values chosen in M5/layout"; these are proposals and nothing is fitted.
2. The shunt on each COM jumper is fitted 2-3 (PNP) by default; change if most sensors are NPN.
3. The accessory symbols for the relays and fuses (K701-K708, FU701-FU708) carry the field `LCSC = LOCAL (India)` and are marked DNP so they stay out of the assembly BOM.
